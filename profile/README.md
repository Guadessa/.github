# Guadessa

### A taste of Spain, thoughtfully brought home.

Guadessa is an early-stage commerce project exploring a simple idea: help people rediscover the Spanish food they enjoyed on their travels, with clear product information and a purchasing experience they can trust.

## The experience we are building

An inviting storefront for a curated assortment of Spanish food, with product stories, ingredients and allergens available before purchase. The first pilot is intended to focus on one approved delivery route, so the buying experience can be backed by stock, handling and support processes that work together.

## Our approach

- **Shopify at the core.** A focused storefront built on established commerce capabilities.
- **Provenance with evidence.** Product claims and food information reviewed before publication.
- **Care beyond checkout.** Traceability, delivery exceptions and customer support designed into the operation.
- **Learn before expanding.** Use a limited pilot to understand demand and delivered-order economics.

## Here on GitHub

This organization is home to Guadessa's product documentation and development work. The project is in planning; assortment, launch destination and availability are still being validated.

No launch date or delivery coverage is announced here. Public information will be updated as decisions are confirmed.
